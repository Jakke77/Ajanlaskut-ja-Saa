## Lopullinen luontikuvaus

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: finished Ubuntu desktop wallpaper, landscape 16:9, ideally 3840 x 2160.
Primary request: an elegant dark copper, gothic-celestial Ubuntu wallpaper matching the Finnish desktop app "Ajanlaskut ja Sää" and its Kupari theme.
Scene/backdrop: near-black charcoal and deep plum night, soft atmospheric haze, sparse delicate stars. Extremely faint concentric orbital arcs and astronomical timekeeping geometry suggest calendars and the passage of time; restrained and sophisticated, not a busy diagram.
Subject: one beautiful recognizable Ubuntu Circle of Friends emblem, accurately formed from the broken circular ring and three round nodes, sculpted in aged brushed copper with softly glowing warm edges, set within a subtle ornamental celestial medallion. Use fine gothic engravings on the outer metal, but keep the Ubuntu symbol itself clean and unmistakable.
Composition: cinematic widescreen desktop background. Place the main emblem left of center, around 40% of the image width and 48% of its height; about 32% of image height. Detail and copper light concentrated around this focal point. Keep the entire rightmost 35% calm, very dark, low contrast, with no prominent shapes, no bright stars, and no ornament behind the desktop clock and weather widgets. Leftmost 7% and upper edge also dark and quiet for Ubuntu dock and top bar. Gentle atmospheric continuity across the whole canvas; no hard panel divisions.
Palette: background #100e15 and #1e1822, engraved dark copper #68483e, luminous accents #ef986e, very sparing soft warm highlights #f6e5db. Overall predominantly dark with refined warm copper illumination.
Style: premium cinematic digital artwork, detailed tactile patinated metal, subtle dimensional lighting, balanced mysterious autumn-night mood, beautifully finished wallpaper rather than a UI mockup.
Constraints: only the wallpaper, not a screenshot. No windows, no widgets, no weather graphs, no clock numbers, no captions, no lettering, no watermark, no borders. No oversized white glow. No distracting objects in the right widget area. Opaque background.
