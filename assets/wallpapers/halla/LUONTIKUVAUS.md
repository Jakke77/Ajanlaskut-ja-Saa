## Käytetty kehote

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: finished Ubuntu desktop wallpaper for the Finnish calendar and weather application Ajanlaskut ja Sää, Halla-yö blue theme. Create a NEW, exquisitely detailed cinematic 16:9 landscape artwork. Scene: a frost-blue celestial night, dark navy-black space with delicate silver stars and subtle blue nebula mist. Subject: one large recognizable Ubuntu Circle of Friends emblem made of luminous frosted silver and translucent blue ice, encircled by elegant concentric astronomical astrolabe rings and small moon-phase spheres. Composition: the complete circular ornament is centered at about 35 percent of the image width and 50 percent height, occupying the left and central portion, with ample framing. The rightmost 38 percent remains very dark, quiet negative space for desktop calendar and weather widgets; no bright objects there. Texture: fine engraved silver metal, crystalline ice rims, graceful clockwork orbital lines, restrained icy cyan glow; sharp exquisite material detail. Mood: majestic, calm Nordic frost night, sophisticated gothic astronomical design coordinated with blue UI accents. Palette: midnight navy, steel blue, glacier cyan and silver-white, no copper/orange/gold. This is an actual wallpaper image, not a desktop screenshot or interface mockup. No text, letters, numerals, clock digits, GUI, windows, borders or watermark. Opaque full-bleed background.
